What it does

Convert audio

Common inputs including MP3; outputs to uncompressed WAV/AIFF or M4A (AAC/ALAC).

Convert video

MP4/M4V/MOV with H.264/H.265 or ProRes 422/4444 video, and AAC/ALAC/PCM audio via AVFoundation.

Batch friendly

Queue multiple files and let the app work in the background.

Native and efficient

Swift 6 + SwiftUI with AVAssetReader/AVAssetWriter under the hood.

Simple by design

Minimal controls for the most common jobs. No distractions.

Privacy

All processing is local on your Mac. No accounts, cloud, or telemetry.

How it works

  1. Add one or more audio/video files.
  2. Choose the target format and destination.
  3. Click Convert. The engine runs with AVFoundation while the UI stays responsive.
  4. Open the converted files directly when done.
Focused on reliable everyday conversions. Advanced workflows intentionally kept minimal.
